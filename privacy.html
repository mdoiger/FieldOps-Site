FieldOps ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile application that helps oil and gas operations teams manage work assignments, inspections, requests, and operational tasks.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Company name
  • User account information
  • Assigned tasks and task completion history
  • Inspection forms and notes
  • Photos uploaded during inspections or work orders
  • Device information required for app functionality
  • Diagnostic information and crash reports to improve the app

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the FieldOps service
  • Synchronize tasks between users
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Protect the security of user accounts

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only with:

  • Your employer or organization using FieldOps
  • Service providers that securely host or process data on our behalf
  • Government authorities when required by law

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information.

Children's Privacy

FieldOps is intended for business use and is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.
